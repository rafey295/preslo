About Preslo

Preslo started from a small frustration — most free PDF and image tools online ask you to upload your file to a server before doing anything with it. For a lot of documents, that's a bit more trust than the task really calls for. Preslo was built to do the same job without that step: every tool here runs entirely inside your browser, so your files never actually leave your device.

The site was founded by Rafay Jutt, and launched on October 1, 2026, with a simple compress-PDF tool as the starting point. The plan since day one has been to keep adding tools — merge, split, image conversion, and more — in the same spirit: fast, free, and private by default, without accounts or paywalls getting in the way of just getting something done.

What we believe in

Tools should be quick to use without a learning curve. They shouldn't ask for more access to your files than the task requires. And they should work the same whether you're on a laptop, a tablet, or a phone.

Where we're headed

Preslo is still early. New tools, an easier account system, and general polish are all actively being worked on. If there's a tool you'd like to see added, the comment section on the homepage is a good place to mention it.
