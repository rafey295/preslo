Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Preslo is built around a simple idea — the tools you use here shouldn't need to see your files to work. This page explains what that means in practice and what little data we do collect.

Your files

When you compress, merge, split, or convert a file on Preslo, that file never leaves your device. Every tool runs locally in your browser using WebAssembly. We don't have a server that receives, stores, or even briefly touches the documents or images you work with. Once you close the tab, nothing of your file remains anywhere on our end, because it was never anywhere on our end to begin with.

Account information

If you create a Preslo account, we store the email address (or phone number) and password you sign up with, along with the date you registered. Passwords are never stored as plain text — they're hashed before they're saved. We use this information to let you log back in and, over time, to keep track of things like your saved preferences. We don't sell this information or share it with advertisers.

Cookies

Preslo uses a small number of cookies and local storage entries to remember things like your theme preference (light or dark) and whether you've already seen the welcome message. These are functional, not tracking, cookies. If ads or analytics are added to the site in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect that clearly before it happens.

Comments

If you post a comment on Preslo, the name and text you submit are stored so the comment can be displayed to other visitors. Please don't include personal information in a comment that you wouldn't want to be public.

Third-party services

Some tools on this site load open-source libraries from public content delivery networks (like cdnjs) to run in your browser. These are code libraries only — they don't transmit your files or personal data anywhere.

Children's privacy

Preslo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone in that age group.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We'd encourage you to check back occasionally if you're curious.
